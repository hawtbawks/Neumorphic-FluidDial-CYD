Neumorphic UI mockups

Every screen is drawn pixel-for-pixel with the same rules as the panel: LovyanGFX FreeSans fonts, Adafruit-GFX shapes, RGB565 colour and stepped shadows built only from fillRoundRect. What you see here is what the CYD can draw.

System

  • One surface. Everything is extruded from one slate grey; light from the top-left.
  • Raised = you can press it. Inset = a readout, a field, or the selected option.
  • Orange means focus. Selected toggle, dial target, primary action. Nothing else.
  • State colour lives in the header chip on every screen: teal Idle, green Run, amber Hold, red Alarm.
  • Home is always bottom-left. Back sits beside it on sub-screens.
  • Start and Stop are the only solid-filled buttons.

Cost on the ESP32

  • Raised element: 7 fillRoundRect (depth 3). Inset: 5.
  • Six FreeSans sizes: about 25 KB of flash.
  • Shadows need 3 px of clear space, so the grid uses 8 px gaps and 10 px margins.
  • Captions keep the 5×7 GLCD font: it is crisper than FreeSans at 8 px.
